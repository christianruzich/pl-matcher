Premier League Finish Matcher

Enter a run of league finishes. The page finds the Premier League team whose stretch of seasons comes closest to it, from 1992-93 to 2025-26.

Whole numbers from 1 to 22, separated by commas or spaces. 1 is first place.

Final tables are built from match results, with the known points deductions applied (Middlesbrough 1996-97, Portsmouth 2009-10, Everton and Nottingham Forest 2023-24). 1992-93 to 1994-95 had 22 teams. The 2025-26 table comes from the same match data and has not been checked against an official table. Match results come from the public-domain (CC0) datasets footballcsv/england and openfootball/england.
